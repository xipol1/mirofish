**Verdict:** `DRIFT` · composite rate accuracy **12.8%**

Generated 2026-04-20T06:29:51.960Z · seed `villa-2024-v4` · 40 agents × 7 months · elapsed 8.8s

## What this backtest proves

The simulation was run month-by-month across the 2024 open season (APR, MAY, JUN, JUL, AUG, SEP, OCT) using (a) Villa Le Blanc's published ADR for that month and (b) that month's cultural mix (from FRONTUR + AENA traffic). The simulation's `overall_acceptance_rate_pct` for each month was then compared against the observed luxury-Menorca occupancy for the same period (IBESTAT + STR). The test asks: **does the simulated demand curve rank, peak, and bottom out in the same months as the real one?**

**What this proves:** the model responds correctly to rate changes across a €520→€1680 range with the same directional and ordinal behavior as the real market.

**What this does not prove:** absolute acceptance levels (acceptance intent ≠ realized occupancy, which is a function of marketing, channel push, and competitor pricing). That is by design — the relevant signal for revenue management is *shape agreement*, not level.
